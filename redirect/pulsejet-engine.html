Lockwood–Hiller Pulsejet Engine

Propulsion & Computational Fluid Dynamics

Project Overview

This project focuses on the design and transient flow analysis of a valveless Lockwood-Hiller Pulsejet Engine. Unlike traditional engines, the Lockwood-Hiller utilizes acoustic resonance and geometric shaping to control the combustion cycle, eliminating moving parts and increasing reliability for high-speed UAV applications.

The analysis captures the complex internal pressure oscillations and velocity gradients. By utilizing SolidWorks Flow Simulation, the study tracks how the pressure wave travels from the combustion chamber through the U-bend and exhaust, effectively sucking in a fresh charge of air through the intake.

Simulation Goals & Convergence

To ensure physical accuracy, multiple Global and Surface Goals (GG/SG) were monitored throughout the 0.1s physical time duration:

  • Average Total Pressure Monitored
  • Average Fluid Temperature Monitored
  • Mass Flow Rate (Exhaust) Achieved (IT = 199)
  • Average Static Pressure Achieved (IT = 25)

Solver Parameters
